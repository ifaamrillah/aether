# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Aether is an open project for contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and viewpoints. Participation should be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence and good faith.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private project data, credentials, or personal information.
- Make space for newcomers and contributors with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Encouraging unsafe use of the project or bypassing its documented safety boundaries.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when doing so is safe and practical.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private reporting/contact mechanisms. Include relevant links and context. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the Aether project in public spaces.
