# Engineering Research

## Objective

Answer a defined question with current, traceable evidence and a recommendation appropriate to the repository.

## Procedure

1. Write the exact research question and decision it supports.
2. Prefer official documentation, specifications, release notes, source repositories, and maintainer statements.
3. Check publication/update dates and the version that applies to this project.
4. Use secondary sources only to add context, not to replace primary evidence for compatibility or behavior claims.
5. Compare at least two viable options when a choice is being made. Include maintenance, security, platform compatibility, complexity, and migration costs.
6. Verify important claims against the source text. Distinguish confirmed facts from interpretation.
7. Record URLs, access dates, version numbers, and unresolved uncertainty.

## Deliverable

Produce a concise research brief with:
- Question and decision context.
- Findings with source citations.
- Version/date applicability.
- Options and trade-offs.
- Recommendation and rationale.
- Remaining uncertainty.

## Guardrails

- Never fabricate documentation, benchmarks, versions, API signatures, or citations.
- Do not treat search snippets as sufficient evidence for a material technical claim.
- Do not copy large sections of third-party documentation; summarize and link to the source.
- Do not assume a package or platform feature exists merely because a blog mentions it.
- For security guidance, prefer vendor advisories and recognized standards.
