# UI/UX Research

## Objective
Ground interface decisions in product context, user tasks, existing design language, and verifiable references before wireframes or prototypes.

## Procedure
1. Identify the user, primary task, usage context, device constraints, and expected outcome.
2. Inspect existing navigation, components, tokens, and interaction patterns.
3. Map the current journey and identify friction, missing states, and confusing transitions.
4. Research platform conventions and comparable products when external context materially improves the design.
5. Separate observed facts, sourced references, assumptions, and hypotheses.
6. Consider keyboard, assistive technology, reduced motion, localization, responsive layouts, and low-bandwidth conditions.
7. Identify loading, empty, error, success, disabled, permission-denied, and partial-data states.
8. Define design goals and observable usability signals.
9. Record source titles, URLs, access dates, and the decisions each source informs.

## Deliverable
A research brief with users and jobs, existing journey, friction, constraints, references, accessibility/responsive considerations, hypotheses, and open questions.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent participants, quotes, analytics, or usability findings.
- Do not copy distinctive competitor branding or screen composition.
- Do not treat visual trends as evidence of usability.
- Do not start production implementation before the design direction and specification are approved.
